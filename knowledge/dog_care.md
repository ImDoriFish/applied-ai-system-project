# Dog Care
General daily, weekly, and yearly care guidance for puppies and adult dogs. Not a substitute for advice from a veterinarian.

## Dog feeding
Puppies: feed 4 meals a day at 8 to 12 weeks old, 3 meals a day at 3 to 6 months, and 2 meals a day from 6 months to 1 year. Adult dogs (1 year and older): 1 meal a day is usually enough, but larger dogs or dogs prone to bloat do better with 2 smaller meals a day. Extras like cooked egg or vegetables should be no more than 10% of daily food. For example, a puppy aged 3 to 6 months could eat at 07:00, 12:00, and 17:00, and an adult dog on 2 meals could eat at 07:00 and 18:00.
Source: [ASPCA, "General Dog Care"](https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/dog-care/general-dog-care)

## Dog water and habitat cleaning
Clean, fresh water should be available at all times. Wash food bowls and scoops with soap and hot water after each use, and wash the water bowl frequently. Give the dog a warm, quiet, draft-free bed or crate with a clean blanket, and wash the bedding often. Outdoor dogs need shade and cool water in hot weather and a warm, dry shelter in cold weather. Pick up your dog's poop on every walk in public places.
Source: [ASPCA, "General Dog Care"](https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/dog-care/general-dog-care)
Source: [U.S. FDA, "Tips for Safe Handling of Pet Food and Treats"](https://www.fda.gov/animal-veterinary/animal-health-literacy/tips-safe-handling-pet-food-and-treats)

## Dog exercise and enrichment
Every dog needs exercise every day, but the amount depends on breed, age, and health. High-energy breeds need much more than low-energy breeds. Puppies should get several short walks or play sessions spread through the day, not one long walk. Senior dogs still need daily, gentler exercise. Mental exercise (training, puzzle games, scent work, fetch, tug) is also important. For example, a simple routine could be walks at 07:30 and 18:30, plus a short training or play session at 13:00. Ask your vet how much activity suits your dog.
Source: [American Kennel Club, "How Much Exercise Does a Dog Need Every Day?"](https://www.akc.org/expert-advice/health/how-much-exercise-does-dog-need/)
Source: [ASPCA, "General Dog Care"](https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/dog-care/general-dog-care)

## Dog grooming
Brushing: once a week for short coats; daily for long or easily matted coats. Bathing: at least once every 3 months (more if the dog is often outdoors or has skin problems). Teeth: brush 2 to 3 times a week with dog toothpaste (never human toothpaste), and check gums and teeth once a week. Nails: trim when they almost touch the ground or click on the floor; for some indoor dogs this can mean weekly. Check ears regularly for wax, smell, or redness.
Source: [ASPCA, "Dog Grooming Tips"](https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/dog-care/dog-grooming-tips)

## Dog health care and vet visits
Puppies: first vet visit at about 6 to 8 weeks old, then about every 3 to 4 weeks until about 4 to 5 months old for vaccines and parasite care. Adult dogs: a wellness exam at least once a year (mature adults once or twice a year). Senior dogs: at least twice a year. Check for fleas and ticks daily in warm weather. Spay or neuter by about 6 months of age. Never give medicine your vet did not prescribe.
Source: [American Animal Hospital Association (AAHA), "How Often Should My Dog Go to the Vet? A Dog Life Stage Guide"](https://www.aaha.org/resources/how-often-should-my-dog-go-to-the-vet-a-dog-life-stage-guide/)
Source: [ASPCA, "General Dog Care"](https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/dog-care/general-dog-care)
