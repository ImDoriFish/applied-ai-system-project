# Fish Care
This file covers routine daily, weekly, and monthly care for freshwater aquarium fish such as bettas and goldfish. Not a substitute for advice from a veterinarian.

## Fish feeding
Feed bettas and goldfish once a day, giving only what they can eat in under two minutes. Overfeeding is the most common mistake: it causes obesity, and uneaten food rots and fouls the water, so remove leftovers daily. Use a quality betta pellet or goldfish flakes and granules, with frozen foods like brine shrimp or daphnia as an occasional treat. Fancy (short-bodied) goldfish should get sinking pellets, and freeze-dried food should be soaked first. Buy food in small amounts; dried food should not be kept more than about 2 months once opened. For example: 08:00 feed one small portion, 08:02 net out anything not eaten.

## Fish water and habitat cleaning
Change 10-25% of the tank water every week and vacuum the gravel with a siphon at the same time. Small bowls or unfiltered betta tanks need more, up to about 50% per week. Always treat new tap water with a water conditioner (dechlorinator) before adding it. Test ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, and pH every 1-2 weeks; in a new tank, test every 2-3 days for the first 6 weeks. Rinse the filter media in a bucket of old tank water (never tap water) once a month, or sooner if it clogs, and do not over-clean it.

## Fish exercise and enrichment
Give fish a roomy tank: at least 20 litres for a betta (10 litres absolute minimum) and at least 50 litres for goldfish; bowls are too small. Bettas need a heater set to 24-26°C; goldfish do best at 20-24°C. Keep aquarium lights on for no more than 10 hours a day and give at least 8 hours of darkness. Add live or artificial plants and hiding spots (about 50% cover for goldfish) with no sharp edges. Never put a mirror in a betta tank, because constant flaring stresses the fish. For example: lights on at 08:00, lights off at 18:00.

## Fish grooming
Fish do not need grooming. Instead, check the fish, the water temperature, and the equipment (heater, filter) every day. Once a week, wipe algae off the inside glass with a clean aquarium-only sponge or magnetic glass cleaner, clean the glass cover, and top up water lost to evaporation. Never use soap or detergent on anything in the tank. Every so often, take decorations out, rinse them under hot water, scrub dirty spots with a clean brush, and let them drain before putting them back.

## Fish health care and vet visits
Good water quality, a proper diet, and regular cleaning are the best ways to prevent illness. Watch your fish daily for warning signs: not eating, lethargy, fast breathing, loss of color, spots, sores, clamped fins, bloating, or floating or swimming oddly. A healthy fish holds its fins up, has bright color, and swims easily. Keep new fish in a separate quarantine tank for 30-60 days before adding them to your main tank. Routine vaccination is not recommended for pet goldfish. Work with a fish veterinarian or experienced fish keeper to set up a health plan for your species.
